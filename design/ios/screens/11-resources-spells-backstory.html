Backstory
Garrick Thorne was a soldier of the Marchwatch before the company disbanded in disgrace. He carries his old captain's longsword and a debt he doesn't talk about.

He came to the Ash Chapel road chasing a rumor: that the thing his company was paid to bury three winters ago has started digging itself out.

Serif reading view, 16/1.65, 16pt margins. Read-only in v1 (edits happen at creation). Long content scrolls; no cards, no chrome — it should read like a page from a novel.
